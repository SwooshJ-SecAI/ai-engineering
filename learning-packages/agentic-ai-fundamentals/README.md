# Agentic AI Engineering — Fundamentals Study Package

A complete, self-built study package for the fundamentals of **Agentic AI Engineering**, distilled
from the 24-chapter Agentic AI Engineering Handbook. Every concept is presented in four layers —
**what it is**, **why it works** (causal mechanism), **applied** (a realistic scenario), and, for
high-stakes concepts, **fails when** (the invalidation condition) — tagged throughout with the
handbook chapter, a difficulty tier, and a confidence level.

The material teaches the **decision logic** behind agentic systems — why each component exists, what
controls its behavior, and where it breaks — rather than flat definitions. Because the source is a
handbook rather than a weighted certification blueprint, depth and question counts are concentrated
on the load-bearing chapters (the Agent Loop, Tools & Function Calling, Memory, RAG / Embeddings /
Vector Databases, Reasoning Architectures, Multi-Agent Systems, and Security / Guardrails).

## How to study (learn → visualize → test → simulate)

1. **Read the notes**, learning each concept's causal mechanism, not just its definition. Master
   Part II (the agent's machinery — Agent Loop, tools, memory, RAG) first; it is the load-bearing core.
2. **Walk the architectural maps** to internalize how information flows and where decisions and
   failures occur.
3. **Take the quiz**, then read every explanation — including why the distractors are wrong.
4. **Sit the full 52-question practice test**, score against the answer grid, and revisit the chapters
   where you missed the most.

## The recurring principles

Three ideas run through the whole package: **earn the complexity** (start with the simplest design
that works), **ground the reasoning** (retrieve knowledge, don't retrain the model), and **keep humans
accountable** for high-stakes, hard-to-reverse decisions.
