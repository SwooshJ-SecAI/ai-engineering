## Generative AI

**Q:** What does temperature control?
**A:** Randomness — low = deterministic/factual, high = creative.

**Q:** What are embeddings?
**A:** Semantic vectors enabling similarity search and RAG.

**Q:** What is a context window?
**A:** How much text the model can consider at once.

## Adapting Foundation Models

**Q:** The three adaptation methods, cheapest first?
**A:** Prompt engineering → RAG → fine-tuning.

**Q:** Best method for "answer from our current private data"?
**A:** RAG.

**Q:** When to fine-tune?
**A:** To durably change behavior/style when prompt + RAG fall short.

**Q:** Zero-shot vs few-shot?
**A:** Zero-shot = just ask; few-shot = provide examples in the prompt.

**Q:** How does RAG reduce hallucination?
**A:** Grounds answers in retrieved source material.
