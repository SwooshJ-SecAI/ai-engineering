## How To Use These Materials

Work the numbered files in order. The detailed notes establish the vocabulary and the AWS service map; the first-principles file untangles the concepts candidates most often confuse (training vs inference cost, prompt engineering vs fine-tuning vs RAG); quizzes and notecards drill recall; the assessment checks readiness with scenario questions.

## Study Approach

AIF-C01 is breadth over depth. The two highest-value skills are: (1) matching a business problem to the right AWS AI service, and (2) reasoning about when to prompt-engineer vs. retrieve (RAG) vs. fine-tune. These notes emphasize the decision logic, because that is what the scenario questions reward.
