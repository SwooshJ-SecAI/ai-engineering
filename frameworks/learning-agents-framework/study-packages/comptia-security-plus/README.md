## How To Use These Materials

The files are numbered as a learning path. Work them in order:

1. **`01-detailed-notes.md`** — Read first. This is the core knowledge, built from the smallest causal unit upward. Do not skim; each section explains why a control exists before what it does.
2. **`02-first-principles.md`** — After the notes, work the hardest concepts from fundamentals. These are the topics that trip up most candidates because they are usually memorized instead of understood.
3. **`03-quizzes.md`** — Test recall and application. Every answer has an explanation; read the explanation even when you get it right.
4. **`04-notecards.md`** — Rapid review. Use in the final days before the exam for spaced repetition.
5. **`05-assessment.md`** — A scenario-based self-assessment with a scoring guide. Treat it as a readiness check.

## Study Approach

Security+ rewards understanding over memorization. The exam is heavily scenario-based: it gives you a situation and asks for the best action. You cannot pattern-match your way through it if you only memorized definitions. The deconstruction approach in these notes is deliberate — when you understand why a control exists, the scenario questions answer themselves.
